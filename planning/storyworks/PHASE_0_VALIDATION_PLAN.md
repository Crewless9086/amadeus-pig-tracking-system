# Phase 0 Validation Gates and Proposed Phase 1

## Phase 0 decision

Proceed to private Phase 1 only if the owner approves:

- the recommended channel promise;
- the three pilot hypotheses;
- a 12-week maximum duration;
- R7,500 maximum cash spend and R35,000 economic-cost ceiling;
- no publication until a separate canary decision.

## Phase 1 gates

### Go

Continue through all three private pilot packages when:

- each package passes the minimum quality and rights standard;
- pilot one completes in no more than 70 internal hours and R2,500 cash, or has a credible documented learning that justifies one revision;
- the workflow produces traceable sources, claims and asset authority without software;
- the three topics remain inside the approved promise.

After authorised publication, continue the 30-day test when the exact canary has passed rights/disclosure/technical review.

### Revise

Revise once when any of these occurs:

- 71–100 hours per pilot;
- a correctable rights gap forces material asset replacement;
- the title/thumbnail promise cannot be made specific without exaggeration;
- private reviewers understand the facts but cannot state the thesis;
- the production model depends on a skill or asset source not yet secured.

Revision must identify one bounded change and its expected effect.

### Stop

Stop or return for owner decision when:

- projected programme cash exceeds R7,500 or economic cost exceeds R35,000;
- elapsed time exceeds 12 weeks;
- any pilot exceeds 100 hours without a compelling reusable capability;
- reliable sources cannot support the central claim;
- rights cannot be documented;
- acceptable quality requires unauthorised contractor/tool spend;
- the output resembles generic repetitive synthetic content;
- the positioning must materially change to make the topics work.

## Post-publication directional gates

These are learning gates for three videos, not promises or statistically definitive thresholds.

| Metric | Go signal | Revise signal | Stop/pivot signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Impressions | At least one pilot receives meaningful browse/suggested testing beyond direct contacts | Mostly direct/external traffic | No platform testing across all three after packaging revisions |
| CTR | 4.5%+ on meaningful impressions | 2.5–4.49% | Below 2.5% across all three after one packaging revision |
| First 30 seconds | 65%+ retained | 50–64% | Below 50% across all three |
| Average percentage viewed | 40%+ | 30–39% | Below 30% across all three |
| Subscriber conversion | 1%+ of viewers on at least one pilot | 0.3–0.99% | Below 0.3% across all three |
| Production time | ≤70 hours | 71–100 hours | >100 hours |
| Qualitative signal | Specific requests/questions about related systems | General praise without topic pull | Confusion, distrust or “generic AI” perception |

Do not interpret a single pilot or a small impression pool as decisive. Record sample size and traffic source with every metric.

## Evidence required before expansion

No second channel or software build until:

- all three pilots have been produced and, if authorised, measured for 30 days;
- at least two demonstrate audience or qualified customer pull;
- the manual workflow exposes a repeated quantified bottleneck;
- proposed automation has a costed payback and isolated ownership model;
- owner separately approves architecture/integration.

## Exact proposed Phase 1 plan

1. Owner decides positioning, pilots, duration and budget.
2. Build pilot one (Petra) manually and measure every hour/cash cost.
3. Conduct private editorial, fact, rights and packaging review.
4. Apply one bounded workflow revision.
5. Build pilots two and three with the revised workflow.
6. Present all three private packages, actual economics and one publication-canary request.
7. Publish only the specifically approved canary.
8. Record the exact public result and run the approved 30-day measurement plan.

It should expose only current pipeline, budget, revenue, protected decisions, safe autonomous work, stale/unknown facts and expected value relative to farm revenue.
