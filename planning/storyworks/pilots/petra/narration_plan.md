# Petra Narration Plan

## Voice

Calm, curious and authoritative without claiming specialist status. The performance should sound like a thoughtful guide discovering how the system fits together.

## Pace

- Average target: 135–145 words per minute.
- Slow down for the opening flood sequence, the five-layer thesis and uncertainty labels.
- Short pauses after “It was inspection, cleaning, repair and responsibility” and each five-layer conclusion line.

## Pronunciation review

Record verified pronunciations before narration:

- Petra
- Nabataean
- Siq
- Wadi Musa
- Wadi Hremiyyeh
- Muthlim
- Wadi Mataha

Do not record until a human pronunciation reference is checked.

## Narrator options

1. Owner/human narration: lowest cash cost and strongest authorship; requires performance and audio-quality testing.
2. Paid human narrator: requires owner approval and contractor rights agreement.
3. Commercial synthetic voice: requires owner approval of provider/cost/terms and a trust/disclosure assessment.
