## 1. Executive overview

| Item | Current truth |
|---|---|
| Working title | **How Petra Tamed a Flash Flood** |
| Viewer promise | Explain how Petra managed too little water and sudden floods through a layered, maintained water system rather than one miraculous device. |
| Central thesis | Petra’s inhabitants managed the watershed as connected layers that slowed and redirected runoff, conveyed and settled supply, and stored water; the system enabled urban life but depended on maintenance and changed over centuries. |
| Planned runtime | **Exactly 14:20** across the current 20-scene visual plan. |
| Intended audience | English-speaking adults interested in history, archaeology, engineering and practical explanations of how societies functioned. |
| Current state | Private partial text/research package with three small style/comprehension prototypes. No completed video, channel, publication authority or final edit exists. |
| Cash/revenue truth | Cash spend R0; revenue R0. Untracked initial time is not zero economic cost. |

## 5. Claim and uncertainty review

The package contains **17 claims supported through 9 registered sources**.
The [fact ledger](fact_ledger.csv) remains authoritative.

### Strongly supported, with existing limits

- P001–P005, P007–P009, P011–P012 and P015–P016 are recorded as
  `verified` with `high` certainty.
- Their wording controls still prohibit absent-rain framing, unsupported
  dimensions or totals, a single construction moment, unchanged governance,
  exact capacity/population claims and maintenance-free performance.

### Careful wording required

- **P006 (`verified`, `medium-high`):** check structures could slow runoff;
  do not assign one purpose to every terrace.
- **P010 (`verified`, `medium-high`):** say the analysis identifies settling
  features; do not universalise the finding.
- **P013 (`verified`, `medium-high`):** the garden-and-pool evidence is one
  elite example; do not generalise it to all residents.
- **P014 (`verified`, `medium`):** describe regional knowledge and local
  adaptation without superiority or isolated-invention claims.

### Interpretation and uncertainty

- **P017 (`inference`, `medium-high`)** is the episode’s synthesis: Petra
  managed water as a layered watershed system rather than through one device.
  It is not a recovered ancient master plan.
- P016 requires explicit gaps in historical rainfall, runoff and component
  chronology.
- Whole-system sequencing, hydrological performance, technology transfer and
  long-period continuity require the labels and qualifiers in the
  [fact-check](fact_check.md).

### Human review gate

A human specialist/editorial read is still required for source interpretation,
attribution across periods, pace, repetition, terminology and pronunciation.
This packet does not perform or replace that review.
