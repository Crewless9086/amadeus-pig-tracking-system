No music or sound asset has been selected, downloaded, generated or licensed.

## Direction

- Restrained, textural score; avoid stereotyped “Middle Eastern” musical shorthand.

## Gate

Before edit, record source, creator, exact licence, commercial/platform permission, modification permission, attribution, acquisition date, evidence location and Content ID implications.

Preferred no-spend test: rights-verified YouTube Audio Library material or an original owner-created bed, subject to current terms and suitability review. Availability is not yet verified and this is not approval to acquire or use a track.
