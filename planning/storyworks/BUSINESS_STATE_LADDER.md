# StoryWorks Business-State Ladder

## Rule

Amadeus StoryWorks is a standalone YouTube media enterprise. The Chronicle
Vault is its first validation property, not necessarily the permanent limit of
the business. Every property, episode and reporting period must preserve its
own evidence, costs and revenue truth.

Missing evidence is `Unknown`. A later state may never be inferred from an
earlier state. Views, subscribers, completed videos, private packages, ChatGPT
or other AI output, projected RPM and platform estimates are not cash and do
not prove commercial success.

## Durable ladder

| Order | State | Minimum evidence | What it does not prove |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Researched | Dated sources, claims, uncertainties and opportunity hypothesis | Production, demand or revenue |
| 2 | Privately produced | Identified private package or media artifact and production record | Approval, publication or audience proof |
| 3 | Rights/fact/quality approved | Recorded rights, factual, editorial and technical approvals for the exact package | Owner publication authority |
| 4 | Owner-approved for publication | Exact owner decision naming package, identity, scope and conditions | Publication actually occurred |
| 5 | Published with exact platform identity | Exact channel, video ID/URL, visibility and platform timestamp | Meaningful performance or monetisation |
| 6 | Platform performance observed | Timestamped platform evidence with sample size and traffic source | YPP eligibility or revenue |
| 7 | YPP eligible | Current platform evidence that the exact channel meets an applicable eligibility route | Application acceptance or monetisation activation |
| 8 | Monetisation activated | Accepted platform/account state for the exact earning features | Revenue earned, finalised or paid |
| 9 | Platform revenue estimated | Platform estimate tied to channel, period, currency and retrieval time | Final revenue or receivable cash |
| 10 | Platform revenue finalised | Platform-finalised amount and period | Payment received or profit |
| 11 | Cash received | Bank/payment evidence reconciled to the platform remittance | Costs covered or distributable profit |
| 12 | Direct costs reconciled | Received cash matched against direct production, tools, rights, contractors and operating costs | Reserve funded or profit distributable |
| 13 | Operating reserve funded | Owner-approved reserve policy and reconciled reserve balance | Authority to transfer surplus |
| 14 | Distributable profit owner-approved | Legally available surplus, tax/accounting treatment and exact owner allocation decision | Transfer completed |
| 15 | Funds transferred and reconciled | Payment/bank evidence, receiving account, purpose and both-side reconciliation | Permission for later transfers |

## Proof classes

- **Audience proof** starts only with exact published-platform evidence and
  must report impressions, viewing and returning interest without converting
  them into revenue claims.
- **Production proof** covers repeatable quality, rights, time and cost. It can
  exist privately and does not prove audience demand.
- **Monetisation eligibility** is a platform threshold state, distinct from
  acceptance into YPP and activation of earning features.
- **Accepted platform revenue** begins only after the platform finalises the
  relevant revenue period. An estimate remains an estimate.
- **Received cash** requires reconciled payment evidence.
- **Distributable profit** exists only after direct costs, obligations and
  operating reserve are reconciled and the owner approves the exact amount.

## Allocation waterfall

No commingling or allocation occurs before revenue is real, received,
reconciled and legally available.

1. Cover StoryWorks direct production, tools, rights and contractor costs.
2. Fund the owner-approved StoryWorks operating reserve.
3. Retain or reinvest funds required for a self-sustaining StoryWorks
   production and operating account.
4. With a separate owner decision, contribute an exact amount toward CHARLIE
   operating costs.
5. With a separate owner decision and applicable legal/tax/accounting review,
   allocate an exact amount toward another named Amadeus venture.

Neither an allocation policy nor an approved amount proves transfer. Every
transfer requires bank/payment evidence and reconciliation. StoryWorks revenue
may not be silently attributed to another venture, and another venture may not
silently subsidise StoryWorks.

## Delivery vocabulary

`Built` means a reviewable candidate exists in its stated scope. For repository
documentation it does not mean a video was privately produced; for a media
artifact it does not mean rights approval, publication, YPP eligibility,
monetisation, revenue, cash receipt or operational business proof.

`Operational` requires evidence from the exact real-world capability being
claimed. Documentation availability, a completed package, a published video,
platform performance, YPP state and financial settlement remain independent.
