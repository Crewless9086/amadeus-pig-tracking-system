# StoryWorks Pilot Unit Economics

## Model rules

- Currency: South African rand (R).
- Owner time is valued, not treated as free.
- RPM means revenue per 1,000 monetised views and is a planning scenario, not a forecast.
- Planning conversion for RPM scenarios: R18 per US dollar. This is a fixed modelling assumption, not a current exchange-rate claim.
- Break-even monetised views = **production cost ÷ RPM × 1,000**.
- Pre-YPP views generate no StoryWorks ad-revenue share.

The lean case assumes owner narration or an already licensed voice workflow, mostly original diagrams/public-domain material, no paid editor and strict scope. It still values internal labour.

## RPM and break-even sensitivity

| RPM scenario | RPM in model | Lean cost views | Base cost views | High cost views |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Low: US$2 | R36 | 239K | 714K | 1.575M |
| Base: US$4 | R72 | 119K | 357K | 788K |
| High: US$7 | R126 | 68K | 204K | 450K |

These are monetised views after YPP eligibility. They exclude tax, revenue-share changes, non-monetised views, payment timing and channel-level overhead. The result is clear: ad revenue alone is unlikely to repay premium pilot production quickly.

## Three-pilot programme ceilings

Recommended validation ceiling:

- **Cash ceiling:** R7,500 total through private package completion, unless the owner approves a specific exception.
- **Economic-cost ceiling:** R35,000 total, including valued internal time.
- **Time ceiling:** 12 calendar weeks from owner approval.
- **Per-pilot target:** no more than 70 internal hours and R2,500 cash before publication approval.

This is deliberately below the base scenario. The purpose is to discover whether quality can be achieved with an original-diagram, rights-light workflow before contractors or paid visual-generation volume.

## Revenue bridge implication

Owned-media economics have a long qualification and payback path. After two portfolio-quality pilots, a narrowly scoped documentary brand-story service could produce earlier cash flow, but only if pricing covers real internal time.

Illustrative service scenarios—not an offer:

| Package price | Estimated internal economic cost | Gross margin before overhead |
|---:|---:|---:|
| R18,000 | R14,000 | 22% |
| R28,000 | R17,000 | 39% |
| R40,000 | R22,000 | 45% |

The service should not be considered commercially ready until pilots demonstrate quality, effort is measured, rights terms are drafted and the owner approves scope/outreach.
