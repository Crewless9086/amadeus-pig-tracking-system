# StoryWorks Business Charter

## Purpose

Determine whether Amadeus StoryWorks can become a commercially viable research-led media business without distracting from farm revenue, livestock operations, existing agents or their reliability programme.

## First 90-day commercial objective

Produce three private, portfolio-quality Chronicle Vault pilot packages within an approved time and spend ceiling, then seek owner approval for a controlled publication test.

This objective separates four kinds of proof:

| Proof | What would count |
|---|---|
| Audience proof | Public pilots attract qualified impressions, clicks, sustained viewing and returning interest after authorised publication. |
| Production proof | Three distinct packages meet the quality/rights bar within the target hours and cost. |
| Revenue proof | Actual YPP income or an owner-authorised, qualified client signal; documents and finished files are not revenue proof. |
| Long-term value | A repeatable topic pipeline, recognisable promise, owned audience and reusable production capability. |

## Two lanes

- **Lane A — owned media:** validate The Chronicle Vault only.
- **Lane B — revenue bridge:** after at least two strong pilots, design—but do not sell—a narrow service: research-led documentary brand stories for farms and agricultural businesses.

## Authority

Safe autonomous work is limited to private research, analysis, documents, scripts, provenance records, pilot packages and decision recommendations.

Owner approval is required for material positioning, final pilot selection, spending, public channel/account creation, publishing, contractors, licences/contracts, prospect contact, offers, paid promotion, AdSense, deployment, repository integration, and any CHARLIE/CORE/farm-system action.

## Non-goals

- No Night Library or Amadeus Farm Tales launch.
- No general-purpose agency.
- No software, modules, APIs, dashboards, agents, databases or migrations.
- No live CORE mission.
- No claim that output completion proves commercial viability.
