# HERDMASTER Unified Breeding Capture Mission

Status: owner-approved plan; implementation must follow the Channel-Invariant
Canonical Action Standard.

## Business outcome

Charl can record normal natural breeding through the application, typed Oom
Sakkie, Telegram or voice. Equivalent facts create one identical protected
preview and one canonical set of exposure records.

## Correct meaning

The normal action is natural boar exposure, not proof of one exact service,
conception or pregnancy date. Exposure start, planned removal and actual
removal are facts. Exact service and conception remain Unknown unless
separately observed and governed.

## Shared contract

One versioned preview accepts exact sow/boar identities, exposure start,
planned removal or the documented 17-day default, shared group identity and
size, optional notes, optional explicit pen effects, and source provenance.

It displays the natural-exposure default, removal date, group membership,
expected farrowing range, every proposed exposure and movement, and all Unknown
facts.

## Same service for every channel

- **Application:** `Teken Natuurlike Plasing Aan`; select a boar, multiple sows,
  start date, optional pen effect and notes, then preview.
- **Typed browser Oom Sakkie:** normalize natural text into the same contract.
- **Telegram:** normalize natural grouped English/Afrikaans into that contract.
- **Voice:** send its transcript through the typed semantic adapter; no separate
  breeding rules or writes.

All use the reconciled grouped exposure preview/execution backend. No channel
writes directly to mating events or private notes for this action.

## Exact service remains separate

Retain a clearly labelled `Teken Presiese Diens Aan` action for a genuinely
known hand mating, AI service or exact observed service date. Natural exposure
must never silently call this path.

## Movement boundary

Exposure does not prove movement. Optional pen effects must be stated or
selected, resolved to canonical pens and displayed separately. No pen supplied
means no movement. A current placement creates no duplicate movement.

## Group intelligence

Each sow retains an individual append-only exposure history while members share
a stable group identity. HERDMASTER may analyse group size/duration, completed
or interrupted exposure, pregnancy and farrowing evidence, litter survival,
weaning, growth, repeat service and boar outcomes across groups.

Group size is evidence, not causality. HERDMASTER must show sample size,
counter-evidence and confounders before suggesting a capacity limit.

## Expected-date handling

Expected farrowing `Vanaf` uses exposure start plus the governed gestation
basis. `Tot` uses actual removal, or planned removal while open, plus that basis.
Application, paper, Telegram and worklists must show the same range. Only exact
service evidence permits an exact expected date.

## Delivery plan

1. Inventory `/master/matings`, the application form, grouped exposure APIs,
   Telegram lifecycle and browser/voice semantic paths.
2. Reconcile legacy history without rewriting it.
3. Make grouped exposure the shared action for natural placement.
4. Add application preview/execute and group/history reads.
5. Route typed, Telegram and voice adapters into that service.
6. Show open/removal state and farrowing range in breeding and pig histories.
7. Keep exact-service capture separate.

## Acceptance

Use one genuine group to prove equivalent application and Oom Sakkie previews;
exactly-once execution; immediate cross-channel canonical readback; movement
only when explicit; no invented service, conception or pregnancy; removal of
the same exposure identities; conservative HERDMASTER use; and zero-effect
replay/concurrent confirmation.

The mission remains outcome-bound until the real cross-channel loop succeeds.
