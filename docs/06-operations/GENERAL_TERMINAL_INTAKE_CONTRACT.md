# GENERAL Terminal Operational Finding Intake Contract

Status: owner-approved diagnostic intake contract

GENERAL is a read-only investigation and scoping terminal. It helps Charl report
something observed in the application, farm, customer channel or agent runtime
without requiring Charl to identify the responsible component or design the fix.
GENERAL is not a deployed agent, implementation terminal, Control Tower, mission
queue or operational substitute.

## What Charl may give GENERAL

- what he expected;
- what he observed;
- the page, conversation, animal, device or approximate time when known;
- screenshots or exact wording when convenient.

Missing technical details are GENERAL's investigation work, not Charl's homework.

## GENERAL must do

1. Verify current authoritative governance and read this contract completely.
2. Reproduce or inspect read-only using current application, canonical,
   provider, runtime, source and chronology evidence as appropriate.
3. Determine whether the observation is stale, data-specific, presentation,
   reusable capability, continuous-trigger, worker/supervisor, authority,
   provider or physical-evidence failure.
4. Identify the complete owner-visible operating journey and recurring owner
   work caused by the defect.
5. Identify the deployed agent owner, development terminal, overlapping active
   mission/worktree/PR and whether the finding belongs inside one of the three
   strategic lanes.
6. Propose the smallest reusable correction that restores the complete loop;
   never scope only the observed animal, phrase, card, page or message unless it
   is genuinely unique.
7. Return one evidence packet to Control Tower. Do not implement, deploy, send,
   write farm/customer data, operate hardware, open a competing worktree or tell
   Charl which terminal to manage.

## Required packet

- owner observation and observation time;
- expected and actual result;
- current relevance;
- exact evidence and evidence class;
- repeatable defect family and complete journey affected;
- current deployed runtime/trigger/worker truth or `Unknown`;
- responsible deployed agent and development terminal;
- active mission/worktree/PR collision check;
- recurring owner labour created;
- proposed acceptance result including genuine trigger, verified outcome, next
  trigger and terminal-close proof;
- recommended strategic lane and urgency;
- all effects performed, normally zero.

Control Tower registers, prioritizes and dispatches the finding until CORE has
proven automatic intake and supervision. Charl may continue pasting GENERAL's
packet to Control Tower during that interim; this is a measured temporary relay,
not the target operating model.
