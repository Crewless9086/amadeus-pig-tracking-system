A generic `Needs Data`, inspection-only list, or terminal-only artifact is not the outcome.

## Clarified farm meaning

For this mission, "best pedigree mix" means the best evidence-backed mating combination using the farm's recorded breeding and production evidence: prior sow-boar services, attributable litter results, born-alive and weaned survival, offspring growth, repeated-pair history, boar workload, health, and every known biological relationship.

Exact biological dam and sire links remain valuable ancestry evidence. Missing parentage for foundation animals must be disclosed, but it is not a universal exclusion. Known direct ancestry, a known shared parent or ancestor, conflicting identity, and a proven unsafe relationship remain hard exclusions. HERDMASTER must not invent ancestry.

## Pen clarification

No pen identifies reproductive state or breeding eligibility by itself. D3 currently happens to contain several females awaiting attention; it is not the mating, resting, recovery, or eligibility pen contract. Current location is used only to find and route an animal after its reproductive state is determined. A missing, stale, or corrected pen must not erase otherwise supported reproductive evidence.

## Female eligibility

A female enters the current mating-review group when canonical evidence supports all of the following:

1. Active, on farm, and retained for breeding.
2. No open nursing litter.
3. No applicable unresolved mating, pregnancy, assumed-pregnancy, expected-farrowing, or inconclusive cycle that prohibits remating.
4. No current medical, welfare, withdrawal, or explicit owner hold that genuinely prohibits mating.
5. Her previous litter is weaned/closed or she otherwise has no active reproductive cycle.

Location alone neither qualifies nor disqualifies her. Missing administrative negative-coverage records must be reported proportionally and must not silence the worklist unless a current conflicting or active hold exists.

Ms Piggy must be assessed using her post-weaning state and freshest supported physical location. Her reported D1 location is not yet canonical and is not itself readiness evidence.

## Pair ranking

For every eligible female, compare Bola, Prince, and Tyson using, where attributable:

- previous pair count;
- born-alive count;
- surviving/weaned count and survival percentage;
- weaning and subsequent offspring-growth evidence;
- adverse mortality or weak-litter signals;
- repeated-pair concentration and useful genetic diversity;
- boar service workload;
- current lifecycle, health, and genuine availability;
- all known relationship evidence.

Return one primary and one reserve boar. Unknown foundation ancestry is a visible limitation, not a zero-score result. Do not infer individual parentage or offspring attribution.

## Farm lifecycle after selection

The operational pattern supplied by Charl is:

1. After weaning and recovery, the selected female is placed with the selected boar and well fed.
2. The exposure period is provisionally about 14 days; this duration still requires confirmation from Charl's father and must not yet be encoded as a permanent rule.
3. The female then moves to the appropriate resting/rebuilding location; no specific pen is yet defined as the universal resting pen.
4. Once the actual mating/service date is recorded, the expected farrowing date is calculated using the existing 114-day rule.
5. HERDMASTER monitors the active cycle and must not recommend another mating while that cycle remains applicable.
6. A proportional pre-farrowing move/preparation action is raised before the expected date.

The recommendation does not itself record or execute a mating. The actual sow, boar, service date, and later movement must be recorded from attributable farm evidence through the governed workflow.
