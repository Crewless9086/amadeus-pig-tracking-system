# Amadeus Meat Cutting And Commercial Standard

Status: owner-confirmed pilot authority
Effective decision date: 2026-07-27
Cutting source: `external_sources/AMADEUS_HALF_CARCASS_CUTTING_STANDARD_v1.0.md`
Cutting document ID: `SAM-MEAT-CUTS-001`

## Authority

The unchanged owner/butcher source document defines the valid cutting routes
for one half pork carcass. This Vault record adds Charl's confirmed commercial,
packaging, full-carcass, payment and delivery decisions.

Together they supersede earlier customer-facing Set A-D menus. Historical
orders and leads retain their original wording. Set D must not be offered for a
new pilot sale and must never be silently converted to another collection.

## Pilot Range

The pilot contains three standard half-carcass collections:

- Set A: **Amadeus Signature Collection**
- Set B: **Amadeus Ember Collection**
- Set C: **Amadeus Grand Cut Collection**

Custom cutting is not offered during the pilot.

Every collection is one complete, butcher-valid cutting route for one half
carcass. Customer-facing descriptions omit internal cut codes.

## Full Carcass

A full carcass consists of two half-carcass selections.

The customer may choose:

- the same collection for both halves; or
- a different collection for each half.

Each half must independently match one complete Set A, Set B or Set C route.
Cuts may not be mixed across incompatible routes within a half.

A pig may be booked for slaughter only when the complete carcass is committed:

- one full-carcass customer has committed and the required deposit is
  bank-confirmed; or
- two half-carcass customers together complete the carcass and both required
  deposits are bank-confirmed.

POP is evidence only and never confirms payment.

## Price And Payment

- Standard half or full carcass price: **R130/kg including VAT**.
- The initial quote is an estimate based on the approved estimated packed
  weight.
- Standard deposit: **50% of the estimated VAT-inclusive total**.
- Final billing uses the butcher's actual final packed weight.
- The bank-confirmed deposit is deducted from the final total.
- The final invoice is sent after final packed weight is recorded.
- The complete balance must be bank-confirmed before delivery.
- No fixed total, packed weight, chop count or package count may be guaranteed
  before processing.

## Delivery

- The pilot is delivery-only.
- Customer collection is not offered because the farm has no approved
  collection facility.
- SAM records the delivery town/area, address or farm name, useful directions
  and delivery notes.
- Delivery timing remains unconfirmed until the complete carcass, deposits,
  slaughter/butcher capacity, final packed weight, final payment and delivery
  capacity have passed their respective gates.
- SAM provides useful, non-spammy milestone updates only through the governed
  customer-communication path.

## Customer Offer Exclusions

The current standard customer offer excludes:

- head;
- feet;
- offal;
- excess bones;
- excess fat;
- excess skin; and
- other butcher trim or unlisted by-products.

These items may be considered in a later owner-reviewed offer if actual demand
and compliant handling justify it. They are not implied by buying a half or
full carcass.

## Cutting And Packaging

- Standard chops, steaks, rashers and other suitable cut pieces are packed
  **four pieces per pack**.
- Pork leg chops are packed **two pieces per pack**.
- Naturally whole cuts remain complete according to the selected collection,
  including whole belly, whole rib, whole leg, half-leg portions and whole
  shanks.
- Stew meat is divided evenly across the applicable packs using the available
  stew meat from that half. Remaining stew meat is distributed evenly rather
  than sold as a guaranteed piece count.
- Exact package weights and quantities are recorded after butchery and are not
  promised before processing.
- The external transport/cold-chain packing method remains separately under
  development and must pass delivery-readiness controls before dispatch.

## Collection Summaries

### Set A - Amadeus Signature Collection

Boneless neck steaks, additional forequarter stew meat, loin chops, bone-in
rib rashers, whole pork belly, two half legs, cut shanks and standard stew
meat.

### Set B - Amadeus Ember Collection

Thick rib chops, shoulder chops, loin chops, whole pork rib, whole pork belly,
one half leg, leg chops, cut shanks and standard stew meat.

Set B is **braai-led**, not braai-only.

### Set C - Amadeus Grand Cut Collection

Neck chops, loin chops, whole pork rib, whole pork belly, whole pork leg, whole
pork shanks (Eisbein) and standard stew meat.

Whole shanks are not represented as cured or smoked unless the butcher has
actually performed that processing.

## Quote Progression

Before a formal estimated quote is generated, SAM must have:

- customer identity and conversation;
- half or full carcass selection;
- one selected collection per half;
- delivery town/area and sufficient delivery context;
- the active R130/kg VAT-inclusive price;
- an approved estimated packed-weight basis;
- the 50% deposit rule; and
- current owner/availability/timing approval required by the quote gate.

The estimated quote must state that:

- weight and total are estimates;
- the final invoice uses actual packed weight;
- the balance is due before delivery;
- slaughter/timing is not confirmed until the full carcass and deposit gates
  pass; and
- delivery timing is confirmed separately.

## Agent Rules

SAM, CHARLIE and supporting agents must:

- use only Set A, Set B and Set C for new pilot sales;
- allow two independently selected collections for a full carcass;
- explain choices before asking the customer to select;
- never invent availability, packed weight, total, delivery date or booking;
- never present excluded by-products as included;
- never use internal cut codes in customer wording; and
- preserve historical Set D evidence without offering Set D to new buyers.
