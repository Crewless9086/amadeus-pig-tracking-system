# Oom Sakkie Telegram message standard

Status: approved owner-facing presentation contract

## Outcome

Oom Sakkie reduces Charl's memory and administration. Telegram messages make
the current decision and usable next step clear in seconds while canonical
evidence, provenance and audit detail remain available behind the message.

## Scheduled farm plan

`TODAY'S FARM PLAN` normally contains:

- no more than three `DO NOW / DO TODAY` items;
- no more than three `COMING UP / WATCH` items;
- at most one grouped owner question when evidence is genuinely missing;
- one short automatic-reassessment line.

Use farm names, animal names, customer/outlet names and business meaning first.
Raw IDs are for disambiguation, deep links and audit evidence, not primary
labels. Every surfaced item names a usable completion route or states plainly
that no owner action is currently available.

## Conversation and specialist answers

An authenticated reply binds to its active provider-delivered question before
generic semantic intake. Preserve supported portions of a partial reply and ask
only the smallest materially missing follow-up. Grouped questions accept grouped
evidence; they do not demand one entity ID unless the original question did.

A direct irrigation, breeding, sales, welfare or marketing request receives the
corresponding specialist answer. A consolidated farm brief is reserved for a
genuinely broad request and must never replace a specific answer.

## Progressive disclosure

The visible message gives the current status, short reason, owner action and
next reassessment. Long evidence, statistics, chronology and provenance belong
in an exact deep link, requested details or the audit surface.

Never expose raw packets, digests, worker states, enum values, database/code
language, internal trigger names, authority boilerplate or implementation
terminology. Clip only at a complete word or sentence boundary.

## Visible events

Use a new visible notification for Started, Completed, urgent welfare and
Intervention outcomes Charl must notice. Do not rely only on a silent edit of
an old card. Ordinary unchanged reassessments remain silent.

## Safety and replay

Presentation grants no farm, customer, payment, publication or hardware
authority. Provider ambiguity is quarantined without retry. Replay and
concurrent workers create zero duplicate messages, edits, questions, work
items or protected effects.
