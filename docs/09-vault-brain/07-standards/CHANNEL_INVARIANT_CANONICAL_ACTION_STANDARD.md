# Channel-Invariant Canonical Action Standard

## Purpose

Amadeus must provide one business capability through every approved owner
interface without creating different meanings or different farm truth.

The application, typed Oom Sakkie, Telegram Oom Sakkie and voice Oom Sakkie are
input and presentation channels. They are not separate business systems. For
the same authenticated owner intent and the same evidence, every channel must
normalize into the same typed canonical action contract, show the same material
preview, execute through the same authority rail and read back the same
canonical result.

## North-star rule

> Speak it, type it or click it: one meaning, one preview, one governed action,
> one canonical record.

Channel differences may improve usability. They may not change business
meaning, required evidence, authority, side effects, idempotency or truth.

## Required architecture

Every protected business capability must have these layers:

1. **Channel adapter** - structured form, typed natural language, Telegram text,
   voice transcript or another explicitly approved interface.
2. **Semantic normalization** - resolve wording into one versioned typed intent
   without granting execution authority.
3. **Canonical preview builder** - load authoritative evidence and produce one
   complete effect preview with stable identity and digest.
4. **Shared validator and authority gate** - apply identical evidence,
   permission, safety and owner-confirmation rules for every channel.
5. **Canonical executor** - execute the exact confirmed preview once through one
   backend action rail.
6. **Authoritative readback** - verify canonical and external provider effects.
7. **Channel presentation** - render the same result appropriately without
   changing its business meaning.

No channel may have a private mutation path, duplicate business rule, shadow
record, weaker confirmation rule or independent interpretation of completion.

## Parity requirements

Equivalent input across channels must preserve canonical identities, action
type, supplied and derived facts, Unknown facts, evidence provenance, proposed
effects, preview digest, owner authority, readback and completion state.

A structured application form may collect facts directly. Natural language and
voice may infer a documented farm default only when the preview exposes that
default and the owner can correct it. Voice transcription is evidence about
what was said, not authority to execute.

## Channel continuity

An owner may start in one channel and continue in another. A Telegram preview
must be visible in the application. An application preview may be confirmed
through an approved Oom Sakkie interface only when identity, digest, expiry and
authority bind exactly. The owner must not repeat facts because the channel
changed.

## Ownership

The specialist owns business semantics and decision evidence. The application
and Oom Sakkie platform own channel adapters and presentation. The canonical
backend owns validation, authority, execution and readback. CORE coordinates
missions but may not create a competing domain contract.

Oom Sakkie routes and presents HERDMASTER, ROOTLINE, SAM, LEDGER, BEACON and
other specialist capabilities; it does not redefine their meaning.

## Safe defaults and explicit effects

Documented defaults may reduce owner work, but previews must expose them.
Optional effects such as movement, customer delivery, payment marking, hardware
control or public posting are never implied by the main action. They remain
separately visible and governed even when included in one atomic preview.

## Testing and completion

Each new or materially changed capability must include a parity matrix for the
application, typed Oom Sakkie, Telegram and voice where enabled. Test Afrikaans,
English, mixed phrasing, grouped input, corrections, replay, channel switching
and ambiguity.

CI and synthetic parity are not Business completion. Completion requires one
genuine owner journey through an available natural channel and one available
structured channel, proving both read the same canonical outcome with zero
duplicate effects. A channel not operational remains explicitly pending.

## Migration rule

Do not rewrite everything at once. When an existing workflow is next changed:

1. inventory all channel paths and writes;
2. identify the specialist contract and canonical source;
3. select or build one shared preview/execute/readback service;
4. put channel adapters behind that service;
5. prove parity before retiring legacy paths;
6. record remaining gaps truthfully.

No new feature may add another channel-specific mutation path after this
standard is adopted.
