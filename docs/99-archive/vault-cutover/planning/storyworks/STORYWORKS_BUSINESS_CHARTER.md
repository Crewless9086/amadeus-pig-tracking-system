# StoryWorks Business Charter

## Purpose

Build Amadeus StoryWorks as a standalone, income-producing YouTube media
enterprise that can become commercially self-sustaining. StoryWorks should
fund its own production and operating accounts first and may later, under
explicit owner-controlled financial governance, contribute toward CHARLIE
operating costs and other named Amadeus ventures.

The Chronicle Vault is the first validation property. It is the current focus,
not necessarily the permanent limit of the StoryWorks portfolio.

## First 90-day commercial objective

Produce three private, portfolio-quality Chronicle Vault pilot packages within
the approved time and spend ceiling, then seek owner approval for a controlled
publication test.

| Proof | What would count |
|---|---|
| Audience proof | Exact published-platform evidence of qualified impressions, clicks, sustained viewing and returning interest. |
| Production proof | Three distinct packages meet the quality/rights bar within target hours and cost. |
| Monetisation proof | Eligibility, accepted YPP/account state and activated earning features are recorded separately. |
| Revenue proof | Platform estimate, platform-finalised revenue, received cash and distributable profit are separate states. |
| Long-term value | A repeatable topic pipeline, recognisable promise, owned audience and reusable production capability. |

The complete sequence is authoritative in
[`BUSINESS_STATE_LADDER.md`](BUSINESS_STATE_LADDER.md). Missing evidence is
`Unknown`; later stages may not be inferred from earlier ones.

## Portfolio and commercial lanes

- **Owned media:** StoryWorks owns long-form YouTube media-business validation
  and production, beginning with The Chronicle Vault.
- **Bounded documentary work:** after at least two strong pilots, StoryWorks
  may design—but not sell without approval—a narrow documentary service. It is
  not a general agency.

BEACON owns Amadeus Farm marketing distribution and campaign evidence. Farm
agents own farm operations. Cross-promotion, shared assets or services require
explicit rights, pricing, attribution and owner approval. Neither BEACON nor
the farm may silently subsidise StoryWorks, and StoryWorks revenue may not be
silently attributed to another venture.

## Revenue governance

Revenue first covers direct production, tools, rights, contractors and
operating reserves. No commingling or allocation occurs before revenue is
real, received, reconciled and legally available. Later CHARLIE or
other-venture funding requires an exact owner-approved allocation and transfer
reconciliation.

Views, subscribers, completed videos, ChatGPT or other AI output and projected
RPM do not equal revenue.

## Authority

Safe autonomous work is limited to private research, analysis, documents,
scripts, provenance records, pilot packages and decision recommendations.

Owner approval is required for material positioning, final pilot selection,
spending, public channel/account creation, publishing, contractors,
licences/contracts, prospect contact, offers, paid promotion, YPP/AdSense
changes, bank/payment details, tax decisions, revenue transfers, funding
allocations, deployment, repository integration, and any CHARLIE/CORE or
farm-system action.

## Intended future CHARLIE relationship — not authorised

CHARLIE may eventually govern pipeline priority, budgets, schedules, evidence,
reviews, protected pauses, performance and business reporting. StoryWorks
must retain its own business goal, accounts, evidence, costs and revenue
truth. CHARLIE must never treat content completion as commercial success.

Future automation requires proven manual bottlenecks, costed payback, stable
production doctrine and separate approval. No CHARLIE/CORE integration is
built, approved, deployed or operational for StoryWorks.

## Non-goals

- No Night Library or Amadeus Farm Tales launch.
- No general-purpose agency.
- No software, modules, APIs, dashboards, agents, databases or migrations.
- No live CORE mission.
- No claim that output completion proves commercial viability.
