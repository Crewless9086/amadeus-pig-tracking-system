# Current YouTube Policy Research

**Checked:** 2026-07-23
**Evidence rule:** Policy claims below use official YouTube/Google sources only. Requirements can change and must be rechecked immediately before channel creation, upload and YPP application.

## Commercial eligibility

South Africa is listed for the expanded YouTube Partner Programme (YPP).

- Earlier-access tier: 500 subscribers, 3 valid public uploads in 90 days and either 3,000 valid public watch hours in 12 months or 3 million valid public Shorts views in 90 days. This tier unlocks selected fan-funding/shopping features, not full ad-revenue sharing.
- Ad/Premium revenue-sharing tier: 1,000 subscribers and either 4,000 valid public watch hours in 12 months or 10 million valid public Shorts views in 90 days.
- Shorts Feed watch hours do not count toward the 4,000-hour route.
- Threshold achievement does not guarantee approval; YouTube reviews the channel as a whole.
- Other prerequisites include an eligible region, no active Community Guidelines strikes, two-step verification, advanced-features access and a linked/ready AdSense for YouTube account.

Sources: [YPP overview and eligibility](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851), [expanded YPP](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13429240), [earning features and South Africa availability](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72857).

## Original, authentic and reused content

YouTube’s channel monetisation policies require original and authentic work. The July 2025 clarification renamed “repetitious content” to “inauthentic content” and explicitly covers mass-produced or repetitive output. Reviewers may examine a channel’s theme, newest and most-viewed videos, watch-time drivers, titles, thumbnails, descriptions and About section.

Repeated format is not automatically disqualifying when the substance of each episode is materially varied and provides educational or entertainment value. Reused material requires significant original commentary, modification or value; permission alone does not guarantee YPP suitability.

Programme consequence: every Chronicle Vault pilot needs an original thesis, human-directed narrative, material episode-to-episode variation, claim ledger, purposeful visuals and documented authorship. A templated script plus generic AI imagery is unacceptable.

Source: [YouTube channel monetisation policies](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392).

## Altered or synthetic content

Creators must use YouTube’s altered-content disclosure when realistic content is meaningfully altered or synthetically generated—for example, making a real person appear to act or speak differently, altering a real event/place, or generating a realistic scene that did not occur.

Idea generation, script assistance, thumbnails/infographics, captions, minor aesthetic edits and cloning one’s own voice are examples that generally do not require the upload disclosure. Realistic historical reenactments made synthetically can require disclosure when viewers could mistake them for real footage. Synthetic music is listed as requiring disclosure.

Disclosure itself does not reduce monetisation eligibility, but repeated failure to disclose can lead to labels, removal or YPP suspension.

Source: [Disclosing altered or synthetic content](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491).

## Copyright and asset rights

- Copyright normally arises automatically in original fixed creative works.
- Credit does not create permission.
- “Only a few seconds” is not a safe rule.
- Permission, a valid licence, public-domain status or a copyright exception may support use, but none guarantees avoidance of a Content ID claim or strike.
- Fair use/fair dealing varies by jurisdiction and is ultimately a legal determination, not a creator checkbox.

Programme consequence: StoryWorks will prefer original assets, commissioned assets with written commercial rights, verified public-domain material and clearly compatible commercial licences. Every external asset requires a provenance record and retained evidence.

Sources: [Copyright on YouTube](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2797466), [copyright tips](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12361994), [common copyright myths](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2797449).

## Advertiser-friendly content

Ad suitability applies to the video, title, thumbnail, description and tags. Relevant risk categories for historical documentaries include violence, shocking content, controversial issues, sensitive events, hateful/derogatory content and unreliable claims. Educational/documentary context matters but is not a blanket exemption.

Programme consequence: avoid graphic thumbnails, sensational treatment of suffering, unsupported “hidden truth” claims and exploitative current-event parallels. Record context, focus, tone, realism and graphicness during QA.

Sources: [advertiser-friendly guidelines](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6162278), [how reviews work](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9269751), [recent guideline updates](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9725604).

## Permissions and account security

Use YouTube Studio channel permissions with least-privilege roles instead of sharing credentials. Invitations expire after 30 days; some capabilities, including YouTube APIs, are not available to invited users. Enable Google two-step verification and retain owner control.

No channel or account will be created during Phase 0.

Source: [YouTube channel permissions](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9481328).

## Phase 0 policy verdict

The proposed channel territory is monetisable in principle, but only if StoryWorks behaves as an authored documentary producer, not a high-volume synthetic-content factory. The compliance burden is manageable through claims, rights, disclosure and final-package ledgers from pilot one.
