# Petra Post-Publication Measurement Plan

No publication is authorised. This plan applies only after an exact canary decision and verified public result.

## Learning objective

Test whether a place-led explanation of a practical historical system can earn qualified clicks and sustained viewing without sensational packaging.

## Baseline record

At publication, record URL, exact timestamp/timezone, visibility, title, thumbnail checksum, description, disclosure, rights approval, runtime, production hours and cost.

## Checkpoints

- 24 hours: delivery/technical anomaly check only.
- 72 hours: early packaging and opening signal; avoid definitive conclusions.
- 7 days: traffic-source, CTR and retention diagnosis.
- 14 days: compare topic/packaging stability.
- 30 days: formal pilot report.

## Metrics

- Impressions and impression CTR.
- Views.
- First 30-second retention.
- Average view duration and percentage viewed.
- Watch hours.
- Subscriber conversion.
- Returning viewers when available.
- Browse, suggested, search, external and direct traffic.
- Comments and specific follow-up questions.
- Production hours/cash/economic cost.
- Cost per watch hour.
- Shorts-to-long conversion if an authorised derivative exists.

## Diagnosis rules

- High CTR + weak 30-second retention: promise/opening mismatch.
- Low CTR + healthy retention: packaging problem.
- Healthy retention + low impressions: insufficient platform testing or weak topic-channel authority.
- Views without subscriber conversion: weak ongoing promise.
- Qualitative requests for related systems: evidence of topic pull.

## Directional gate

Use the approved Phase 0 thresholds, but report sample size and traffic source beside every percentage. Petra alone cannot validate or invalidate the channel.
