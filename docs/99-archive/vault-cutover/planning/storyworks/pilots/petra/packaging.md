# Petra Packaging Alternatives

## Recommended title

**How Petra Tamed a Flash Flood**

Why: concrete place, mechanism and danger. “Tamed” is qualified by the script’s explanation that the system reduced rather than eliminated risk.

## Alternatives

1. **The Water System That Kept Petra Alive**
   - Clear channel-promise fit.
   - Less immediate tension and may imply supply more than flood control.

2. **Petra’s Real Wonder Was Built for Water**
   - Strong contrast with the Treasury.
   - “Real wonder” is more subjective and risks diminishing the monument.

3. **How Petra Survived Too Little Water—and Too Much**
   - Best expression of the paradox.
   - Longer and less object-specific.

## Recommended thumbnail concept

Original simplified overhead schematic of the Siq/Treasury area with a blue flood path splitting at a diversion point.

Text: **TOO MUCH WATER**

Rules:

- Do not show a synthetic wall of water at the Treasury as though it were documented footage.
- Do not use red arrows/circles or “ancient secret.”
- Use a schematic label if geography is simplified.
- Thumbnail must remain legible at small size.

## Alternatives

- Split image: dry Petra / blue runoff network; text **DESERT FLOOD**.
- Close detail of rock-cut channel plus simple flow line; text **THE HIDDEN SYSTEM**. Use only if “hidden” describes overlooked infrastructure, not suppressed knowledge.

## Packaging test

Private reviewers answer before seeing the script:

1. What do you expect to learn?
2. Does the image look like evidence, reconstruction or fantasy?
3. Is any claim misleading?
4. Which title would you click, and why?

## Provisional owner direction — 2026-07-25

- **How Petra Tamed a Flash Flood** remains a working title only. “Tamed” must mean moderated risk through maintained infrastructure, never proof that one undocumented flood was completely controlled or that all flood danger was eliminated.
- The current A011 thumbnail is **revision required**, exploratory and not the final thumbnail.
- Revised directions may test: (a) a recognisable but independently composed Petra/Siq schematic with a bounded runoff/diversion cue; (b) the dry-setting/runoff paradox using original diagrams only; or (c) the five-layer system as the visual subject rather than a dramatic flood.
- Reject fabricated flood imagery, misleading geography, unlicensed photographs/maps and any visual that implies a documented event. Final direction remains owner-gated after factual, rights, small-size and comprehension QA.
