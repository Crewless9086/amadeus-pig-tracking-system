# Petra Altered/Synthetic Content Review

## Current recommendation

**Upload disclosure decision: pending final assets.**

Current research, script assistance, titles, thumbnail planning and original schematic diagrams do not by themselves require YouTube’s altered-content disclosure under the policy reviewed on 2026-07-23.

Disclosure will be required if the final video includes realistic synthetic scenes that could be mistaken for actual Petra, real historical footage or a real event; another person’s cloned voice; or synthetic music under current examples.

## Required in-video labels

Regardless of upload-setting outcome:

- `SIMPLIFIED RECONSTRUCTION`
- `CONCEPTUAL FLOW — NOT A MEASURED EVENT`
- `MODERN HYDROLOGICAL INTERPRETATION`

## Final decision inputs

- Narration source and voice ownership.
- Every visual’s creation/source record.
- Whether any generated imagery looks realistic.
- Music creation/source.
- Final title, thumbnail and description.

Official source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491
