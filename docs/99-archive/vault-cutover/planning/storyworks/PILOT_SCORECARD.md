# Chronicle Vault Opportunity Slate and Pilot Scorecard

## Weighted model

Scores are directional bands, not false precision. Each factor is scored 1–5 and multiplied by its weight.

| Factor | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Demonstrated audience demand | 15% |
| Click potential | 12% |
| Retention potential | 12% |
| Originality | 12% |
| Source quality | 10% |
| Visual feasibility | 10% |
| Production cost efficiency | 8% |
| Monetisation potential | 7% |
| Brand fit | 8% |
| Compliance safety | 6% |

Evidence strength is separately labelled High/Medium/Low. A high score with weak evidence is not production-ready.

## Evidence behind scores

- Demand is supported by large, active history/engineering audiences and repeated performance of “how/why systems work” packaging in the competitor review.
- Originality is higher where the civilian system, maintenance burden or decision trade-off is central, lower for familiar Rome/roads/salt frames.
- Source quality is higher for well-studied archaeological/engineering systems and lower where claims span diverse times/places.
- Visual feasibility favours maps, sectional diagrams, flows and licensed/public-domain site imagery.
- Cost favours episodes that can be explained with diagrams and original motion graphics over extensive cinematic reenactment.
- Monetisation safety favours educational infrastructure/food/logistics topics without graphic violence or current political controversy.

## Three proposed pilots

These are recommendations requiring owner approval because they deliberately test different material hypotheses.

1. **Petra** — tests a strong place, visual engineering and immediate survival stakes.
2. **Rome’s grain system** — tests a complex supply-chain story and familiar civilisation with an unfamiliar mechanism.
3. **Inca roads without wheels** — tests a non-Mediterranean network story and challenges a common assumption.

Together they remain inside one promise while testing water, food/logistics and transport; place-led, system-led and assumption-led packaging; and three distinct visual burdens.

Reserve alternative if rights/source feasibility changes: **Rice terraces that turned floods into farms**.
