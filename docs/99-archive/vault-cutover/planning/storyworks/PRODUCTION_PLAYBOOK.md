# Lean Manual Production Playbook

This playbook is prepared for Phase 1 but is not authority to begin full production or publish.

## Package flow

1. Owner-approved pilot and positioning.
2. Episode brief and research question.
3. Source packet with source-quality notes.
4. Claim-by-claim fact ledger.
5. Original thesis and script.
6. Independent fact-check pass and uncertainty labels.
7. Visual plan built around explanation, not decoration.
8. Asset manifest and rights/AI provenance.
9. Narration, edit, music and sound plans.
10. Three title and three thumbnail alternatives.
11. Description and disclosure recommendation.
12. Copyright, reused-content and advertiser-suitability review.
13. Final QA plus actual time/cost report.
14. Private tested export and publication decision request.
15. Post-publication measurement plan.

## Minimum quality gate

- A defensible original thesis.
- At least three credible substantive sources, including specialist/primary material where practical.
- Every material factual claim linked to evidence.
- No copied language, fabricated facts or unlabeled speculation.
- Coherent narrative with hook, development, consequence and conclusion.
- Purposeful, rights-cleared visuals; no stitched generic AI clips.
- Title/thumbnail accurately represent the episode.
- Audio intelligible and consistent; final export has no visible technical defects.
- Reviewer can identify human creative decisions in research, structure, narration and edit.
