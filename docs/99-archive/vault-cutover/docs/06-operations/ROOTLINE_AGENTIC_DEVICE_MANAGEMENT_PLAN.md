# ROOTLINE Agentic Device Management Plan

Status: reconciled durable doctrine; documentation only. This plan grants no
hardware, provider, deployment, configuration, or production authority.

## Outcome

ROOTLINE manages farm water infrastructure through one scalable,
device-class-aware operating loop. B/C irrigation is the commissioned bounded
foundation, not a special-case planner. Borehole, fertilizer equipment and
future valves, breakers and sensors join only through their own typed contract,
commissioning evidence and authority decision.

Application actions, typed Oom Sakkie, Telegram and voice are channel adapters.
Equivalent owner intent must normalize through the
[`Channel-Invariant Canonical Action Standard`](../09-vault-brain/07-standards/CHANNEL_INVARIANT_CANONICAL_ACTION_STANDARD.md):
one specialist-owned preview, authority gate, executor and authoritative
readback. No channel may create a private ROOTLINE action or weaker safety path.

## Current authority boundary

- B12345 and C12345 are commissioned gravity-fed valves. Their power state is
  minor control evidence; SOC, solar, load, grid and reserve cannot determine
  irrigation need, rank, window or eligibility.
- Current bounded execution is one zone and one segment at a time, no longer
  than 3,599 seconds. A fresh canonical decision, current provider state,
  native auto-OFF and verified shutdown are mandatory. Segment two is a new
  decision and identity.
- Source defaults fail closed: autonomous B/C dispatch requires the exact
  runtime authority flag to be explicitly enabled in a separately assigned
  production window. This documentation audit did not read production
  configuration and does not establish its current deployed value. No enabling
  is authorized here; a commissioned device, source contract or passing test
  is not activation.
- The same zone may be dispatched at most once per Johannesburg operating day.
  The authoritative store must atomically consume the stable zone/date
  eligibility key; regenerated plans, concurrency and replay cannot create a
  second dispatch.
- Provider acceptance is not completion. Provider-confirmed OFF/native
  shutdown evidence and the required physical or authoritative outcome must
  close each execution. Uncertain shutdown fails closed and excludes reuse.

## Device delivery order

1. Preserve one canonical Supabase plan/execution projection across dashboard,
   irrigation detail, Oom Sakkie and provider history; never present legacy
   Sheet rows as current execution truth.
2. Preserve B/C weekly obligation, effective-rainfall evidence, independent
   zone decisions, 3,599-second native fail-stop, deterministic OFF,
   same-zone daily dispatch guard and silent unchanged reassessment.
3. Complete fertilizer mixer and injection as separate auxiliary-device
   capabilities. Mixing is an energy-relevant support task; injection is a
   flow-dependent task bound to exactly one active eligible irrigation zone.
   Neither is an irrigation zone and neither inherits B/C authority.
4. Treat the Borehole 1 SmartLife/IFTTT plug as a separately commissioned
   device. Its electrical command/readback, motor operation, water movement
   and tank outcome are distinct evidence. No B/C or fertilizer authority
   activates borehole control.
5. Add later devices only through the same typed registry, commissioning,
   canonical-action and operational-proof path.

## B/C acceptance

- Four-day-per-zone weekly obligations derive from verified outcome history;
  absent visual need does not erase debt.
- Observed rain, effective-rainfall credit, water availability, season,
  verified history and owner corrections influence planning proportionally.
- Power evidence cannot change B/C need, rank, timing or eligibility.
- Only fresh controller/readback safety evidence may block actuation after the
  water decision supports it.
- One zone runs at a time; each segment is at most 3,599 seconds and requires
  native auto-OFF plus provider-confirmed OFF/shutdown evidence.
- A second segment requires verified shutdown and a fresh canonical decision.
- Same-zone same-day replay or concurrent dispatch creates zero commands.
- Unchanged reassessment creates zero Telegram sends or edits.

## Expansion acceptance

A device is operational only when its typed contract, physical mapping,
provider readback, safe OFF state, native shutdown, dependencies,
commissioning, standing authority, outcome verification and notification
behavior are proven. Explicit owner authorization is required for configuration
or actuation outside standing authority. Physical/provider verification is
required before completion. Missing evidence remains local to that device and
must fail the affected protected action closed without stopping supported
read-only work elsewhere.

## Non-outcomes

This document does not start ROOTLINE implementation, enable autonomy, call
SmartLife/IFTTT/eWeLink, change Render or Supabase, send Telegram, configure a
device, or operate farm hardware.
