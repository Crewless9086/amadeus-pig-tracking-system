# Control Tower Terminal Mission Register

## Purpose

This is the single owner-facing planning and dispatch ledger for the visible development terminals CORE, HERDMASTER, ROOTLINE, OOM SAKKIE, SAM and BEACON. It prevents owner observations, defects and approved plans from being lost while another mission is running or before a terminal is launched.

Charl may report observations and paste terminal feedback without tracking terminal availability or deciding what runs next. Control Tower owns registration, prioritisation, dependency reconciliation and selection of the next eligible mission.

This ledger is planning evidence, not runtime or production authority. A queued item is not delivered to a visible terminal until proven transport exists or Charl pastes the exact dispatched instruction. A terminal is not running merely because a prompt exists.

## Dispatch protocol

For every terminal feedback or new observation, Control Tower must:

1. register the item before offering an execution prompt;
2. identify its deployed capability owner and visible development terminal;
3. reconcile the terminal's active mission, last delivered instruction, worktree and serialized runtime ownership;
4. classify the decision as exactly one of `CONTINUE - SEND NOTHING`, `ADDENDUM`, `NEW MISSION`, `PARALLEL MISSION`, or `WAIT FOR INPUT`;
5. issue no second full mission to a terminal recorded as running;
6. select the highest-value eligible queued mission when the terminal releases;
7. preserve the exact owner-visible acceptance outcome through implementation and production proof;
8. record delivery truth separately as `queued_not_dispatched`, `prompt_prepared`, `delivered`, `acknowledged`, `started`, `progress_observed`, `completed`, or `contained`;
9. keep documented, runtime-loaded, provider-verified and physical facts separate;
10. update this ledger from the next terminal feedback without requiring Charl to remember the queue.
